# TubeMailGorilla Unlocked - Installation Guide

Thank you for choosing TubeMailGorilla Unlocked! Installing it is quick and
easy - the installation wizard handles everything for you.

This edition needs **no account**: there is no sign-in, no subscription and no
feature limits. Everything is unlocked from the moment you open the app.

## What you need
- A Windows 10 or newer computer
- An internet connection (only needed during installation, to download the AI)
- About 5 GB of free disk space
- **Recommended: an NVIDIA RTX graphics card** for the fastest, smoothest AI

## Do I need an RTX GPU?
An **NVIDIA RTX graphics card is recommended** - it makes the AI assistant
noticeably faster and more responsive. That said, it is not required: the app
still runs perfectly well on a regular computer without one, everything works
fully offline either way, and your data never leaves your machine.

## How to install
1. Double-click **TubeMailGorillaUnlocked-setup.exe**.
2. If Windows asks *"Do you want to allow this app to make changes to your
   device?"*, click **Yes** - this is normal.
3. Follow the wizard and click **Next** until you reach **Install**.
4. During installation, the AI assistant is downloaded automatically
   (about 1.9 GB). This is the longest step and depends on your internet
   speed. The AI runs completely on your own computer.
5. When it is finished, TubeMailGorilla Unlocked is ready to use. Launch it
   from the Start Menu or the desktop shortcut.

## What is unlocked
- Unlimited YouTube lead extraction (keyword and channel searches)
- Bulk (CSV) extraction across many keywords at once
- AI-written icebreakers, generated on your own machine
- Unlimited email templates and custom shortcodes
- Unlimited campaigns - send to every lead you have
- Block list, contact manager and unlimited SMTP accounts

## After installation
- No account or internet connection is required to use the app.
- Leads, templates and settings are stored in a local database on this PC.
- If the AI download was interrupted during installation, do not worry:
  TubeMailGorilla Unlocked finishes it automatically the first time it is
  needed.

## How to uninstall
1. Open **Settings -> Apps -> Installed apps** (or Control Panel -> Programs).
2. Find **TubeMailGorilla Unlocked** and click **Uninstall**.
3. The app and the AI assistant are both removed from your computer.

## Troubleshooting
- **The installer was blocked by Windows** - click **More info -> Run anyway**.
- **Installation seems stuck on the AI download** - the file is large, please
  wait; check the progress bar for updates.
- **Anything else** - contact us at our website: https://www.tubemailgorilla.xyz
