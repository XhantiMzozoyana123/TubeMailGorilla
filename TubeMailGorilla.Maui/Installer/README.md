# TubeMailGorilla — Installation Guide

Thank you for choosing TubeMailGorilla! Installing it is quick and easy —
everything is handled for you by the installation wizard.

## What you need
- A Windows 10 or newer computer
- An internet connection (only needed during installation, to download the AI)
- About 5 GB of free disk space
- **Recommended: an NVIDIA RTX graphics card** for the fastest, smoothest AI

## Do I need an RTX GPU?
An **NVIDIA RTX graphics card is recommended** — it makes the AI assistant
noticeably faster and more responsive, giving you the best TubeMailGorilla
experience. That said, it is not required: the app still runs perfectly
well on a regular computer without one, everything works fully offline
either way, and your data never leaves your machine.

## How to install
1. Double-click **TubeMailGorilla-setup.exe**.
2. If Windows asks *"Do you want to allow this app to make changes to your
   device?"*, click **Yes** — this is normal.
3. Follow the wizard and click **Next** until you reach **Install**.
4. During installation, the AI assistant is downloaded automatically
   (about 1.9 GB). This is the longest step and depends on your internet
   speed. The AI runs completely on your own computer — your data never
   leaves your machine.
5. When it's finished, TubeMailGorilla is ready to use. Launch it from the
   Start Menu or the desktop shortcut.

## After installation
- No internet is required to use the AI features — everything works offline.
- If the AI download was interrupted during installation, don't worry:
  TubeMailGorilla will finish it automatically the first time it needs it.

## How to uninstall
1. Open **Settings → Apps → Installed apps** (or Control Panel → Programs).
2. Find **TubeMailGorilla** and click **Uninstall**.
3. The app and the AI assistant are both removed from your computer.

## Troubleshooting
- **The installer was blocked by Windows** — click **More info → Run anyway**.
- **Installation seems stuck on the AI download** — the file is large, please
  wait; you can check the progress bar for updates.
- **Anything else** — contact us at our website: https://www.tubemailgorilla.xyz
